Complete repair kit for electronic component restoration. Includes power supply, connector cables, Absorbent, Nickel, and 24K Gold pen.

About this product

The Contact Repair Kit is the focused subset of the Micro-Metallizer system used by electronics technicians, aerospace MRO crews, and avionics repair shops to restore conductivity to worn or corroded electrical contacts.

The three pens in the kit — Absorbent (for cleaning the contact surface), Nickel (as an underlayer), and Gold 24K (as the final conductive layer) — execute the standard contact-restoration sequence on copper-based connectors, edge connectors, switch contacts, and connector pins.

This kit is significantly more cost-effective than the Deluxe Kit if your work is centered on electrical contact repair rather than general-purpose plating. The Heavy Duty version is available for shops doing higher-volume restoration work.
