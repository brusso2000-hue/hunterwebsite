Replacement connector cables for attaching Micro-Metallizer pens to the power supply.

About this product

Standard replacement connector cables for the Micro-Metallizer pen plating system. The cables connect any plating pen — standard or heavy duty — to the variable DC power supply (PL-1000).

Included with all kits (Deluxe, Contact Repair, Heavy Duty equivalents) and with the standalone Power Supply purchase. This SKU is purely for replacement when an existing cable wears out or becomes damaged.

Compatible with the full Micro-Metallizer power supply range, current and legacy.
